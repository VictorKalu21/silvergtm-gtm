# Open-jobs search: Director+ titles for the supply chain resilience play

Use in the Clay job-posting search column (and in the hiring-signal Claygent) for the Tier-1 scoring list. A company advertising one of these roles is building or rebuilding supply chain, procurement or logistics capability. The v1/v2/v3 resilience prompt excludes hiring on purpose because this column covers it.

Seniority filter: Director and above. In UK companies "Head of" is usually director-level and reports to a Chief or Managing Director, so it is included. Manager, Lead, Senior Manager and Analyst are excluded.

Modifiers to accept on any title: Group, Global, Regional, UK, Europe/EMEA, Interim, Deputy, Associate Director, Senior Director, Executive Director. Treat "Vice President" and "VP" as the same.

## 1. Supply chain (core)

- Chief Supply Chain Officer / CSCO
- Chief Operations Officer / COO (only where the JD mentions supply chain, procurement or logistics)
- Supply Chain Director / Director of Supply Chain
- VP Supply Chain
- Head of Supply Chain
- Head of Supply Chain Operations
- Director of Supply Chain Planning / Head of Planning (demand or supply planning)
- Head of Inventory / Director of Inventory and Availability
- Head of Supply Chain Transformation / Supply Chain Transformation Director
- Head of Supply Chain Resilience / Supply Chain Risk Director

## 2. Procurement and sourcing

- Chief Procurement Officer / CPO
- Procurement Director / Director of Procurement
- VP Procurement
- Head of Procurement
- Head of Group Procurement / Group Procurement Director
- Head of Strategic Sourcing / Director of Sourcing
- Head of Global Sourcing (retail: Pepco Global Sourcing, JD, Halfords buying offices)
- Head of Indirect Procurement / Head of Direct Procurement
- Head of Category Management (goods not for resale or direct categories)
- Head of Supplier Management / Head of Supplier Relationship Management
- Head of Commercial Procurement (housing associations, rail, infrastructure)
- Head of Procurement Transformation

## 3. Logistics and distribution

- Chief Logistics Officer
- Logistics Director / Director of Logistics
- VP Logistics / VP Distribution
- Head of Logistics
- Head of Distribution / Distribution Director
- Head of Warehousing / Director of Warehouse Operations
- Head of Transport / Transport Director (own fleet, not services sold)
- Head of Fulfilment / Director of Fulfilment
- Head of Network Design / Head of Logistics Network
- General Manager, Distribution Centre (only for a newly opened site)

## 4. Supplier governance, risk and ESG

- Head of Supplier Assurance / Director of Supplier Assurance
- Head of Supplier Risk / Third Party Risk Director
- Head of Ethical Trade / Head of Responsible Sourcing
- Head of Human Rights / Modern Slavery Lead (Director level)
- Head of Sustainable Procurement / Director of Sustainable Sourcing
- Head of Scope 3 / Head of Supply Chain Sustainability
- Head of Supply Chain Compliance / Trade Compliance Director
- Head of Customs and Trade / Director of Global Trade

## 5. Sector-specific variants seen on the Tier-1 list

- Retail: Head of Buying Operations, Sourcing Director, Head of Merchandise Planning, Head of Product Supply
- Housing associations: Head of Procurement and Contracts, Director of Assets and Procurement, Head of Commercial (repairs and maintenance supply chain)
- Rail and transport operators: Head of Supply Chain and Procurement, Head of Materials, Fleet Procurement Director
- Construction and infrastructure: Supply Chain Director (subcontractor and materials), Head of Procurement and Supply Chain, Materials Director, Head of Commercial and Procurement
- Foodservice and grocery: Head of Supply Chain Planning, Head of Category Sourcing, Head of Food Safety and Supplier Quality, Head of Depot Operations

## Search strings

For a Clay job-search column or a SERP dork, one query per group, company name substituted:

```
"{{Company Name}}" ("Chief Supply Chain Officer" OR "Supply Chain Director" OR "Head of Supply Chain" OR "VP Supply Chain")
"{{Company Name}}" ("Chief Procurement Officer" OR "Procurement Director" OR "Head of Procurement" OR "Head of Sourcing" OR "Head of Global Sourcing")
"{{Company Name}}" ("Logistics Director" OR "Head of Logistics" OR "Head of Distribution" OR "Head of Warehousing" OR "Head of Transport")
"{{Company Name}}" ("Head of Supplier Assurance" OR "Head of Ethical Trade" OR "Head of Responsible Sourcing" OR "Third Party Risk" OR "Head of Supply Chain Sustainability")
```

Exclusions for the search (titles that match the words but are the wrong signal): "Supply Chain Solutions" (a product name at TVS SCS, Evri, GB Railfreight), "Sales Director Supply Chain", "Business Development Director Logistics", "Account Director", any title at a recruitment agency posting on the company's behalf without naming it.
