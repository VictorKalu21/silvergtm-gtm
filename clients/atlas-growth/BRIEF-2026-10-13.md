Two trial clients from the first month of sending, on a list that was never a finished product. Bounce rate under 1.3% everywhere, so the lists are clean.

## What worked

**UK damp-proofing and structural repair contractors.** 2.0% of everyone contacted replied positively. Owners replied the way owners do: "call me", "send me a WhatsApp", "worth a chat". One meeting turned into "we'd like to go ahead using your service" the same day. This list is fully used; it was the whole UK.

**UK battery and solar installers.** 0.77% positive, two meetings, both from design or sales directors rather than owners. A sizeable list remains.

## What we learned

1. **US mail was not reaching Google-hosted inboxes.** Of 357 US contacts on Google Workspace, zero replied, not even an out-of-office. Of 161 on Microsoft 365, six replied, which is a better rate than either UK campaign. US contractors are mostly on Google, UK trades are mostly on Microsoft, so the UK looked stronger than it is and the US looked weaker than it is. Fix is in progress; until a test lands at Gmail, US sends go to Microsoft-hosted businesses only.
2. **Nobody objects to the price. They object to paying before a job exists.** Five replies said some version of "I'll pay when the customer has signed and paid a deposit." The 10-day trial, where they fund the ads in their own account and only pay per attended appointment, answers that directly, and it was not in the email. The next version leads with it.
3. **Owners want the phone, not a calendar link.** Six positive replies went quiet after being offered a video call. Reply handling now gives the price, the qualification criteria and the trial up front, and asks for a number.
4. **Generator dealers listed by Generac and Kohler already get manufacturer leads.** "I don't need a salesman." Electricians found through Google Maps respond better. The next US batch is Maps-sourced only.
