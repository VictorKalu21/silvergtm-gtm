> We help foundation repair companies book 10 qualified foundation inspection appointments that turn

## What this trade actually is in Australia

"Foundation repair" is a more natural label here than in the UK, because reactive clay soils across
the eastern seaboard, Adelaide and Perth make foundation movement a real residential trade. But the
market self-labels **by state**, and a list built on the American phrase alone misses most of it:

| self-label | where | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **underpinning** | everywhere | the national word for the job; also the NSW licence class ("Underpinning and piering") |
| **restumping / reblocking** | Victoria (and Tasmania, older Queensland stock) | replacing timber stumps under a stumped house with concrete or steel — the same job on a different house type. **Recommended ICP, not adjacent** (operator decision at GATE 1) |
| **resin injection · slab lifting · house levelling · relevelling · foundation repair** | national franchises and their licensees (Mainmark, Uretek, Buildfix, Teretek) | polyurethane / geopolymer injection under slabs and footings |
| **house raising** | Queensland, northern NSW | lifting a house to build under or above flood level. The same firms, but a lift-and-build job — tagged adjacent and judged by the site-text adjudication |
| subsidence repair | shared with the UK | the homeowner's word for the problem |

Waterproofing and damp are **not** the adjacent trade the way they were in the UK: in Australia
"waterproofing" is the bathroom / balcony membrane trade and is kept out of the type allow. The
adjacent trades here are house raising, restumping (if the operator rules it adjacent rather than
ICP), retaining walls and structural engineering; the engineers are a deny, as in the UK.

Share of the universe by self-label: *(probe)*.

## Footprint

**Whole of Australia** (operator to confirm against "east coast plus Perth and Adelaide"),
metro-anchored, not exhaustively tiled. **138 town anchors + 44 densification tiles = 182 tiles**,
zoom 13, single pass, generated by `gen-runsheet-au.js` (never hand-typed; the generator asserts
every latitude negative, every longitude positive, and every tile inside its own state's box).

| state | anchors | densify | tiles |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSW | 40 | 12 (Sydney) | 52 |
| VIC | 30 | 10 (Melbourne) | 40 |
| QLD | 30 | 11 (Brisbane 6, Gold Coast 3, Sunshine Coast 2) | 41 |
| WA | 15 | 6 (Perth) | 21 |
| SA | 12 | 5 (Adelaide) | 17 |
| TAS | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| ACT | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| NT | 3 | 0 | 3 |

Coverage aim: every town above ~30k plus the regional centres, so that no populated coastal area
sits more than ~40 km from a tile and no inland centre more than ~100 km. Known, accepted gaps: the
Pilbara and Kimberley interior, Cape York, the Nullarbor, western NSW beyond Broken Hill, and the
Barkly — no anchor set fixes those without spending on empty viewports. Whole-of-Australia versus
east-coast-plus-Perth-and-Adelaide is a **GATE 1 decision**: dropping TAS, NT, and the WA/SA/QLD
regionals would remove ~45 tiles (~450 rows, ~1,170 Maps calls at the UK rate).

Config: `geo.footprint.mode: "areas"`, `geo.country: "au"`,
`geo.region_from_city: "\b(NSW|VIC|QLD|SA|WA|TAS|ACT|NT)\b"`, `geo.region_default: "Australia"`.
**Unlike the UK, Australia has a state token**, so `footprint-gate.js` runs the region check as well
as the hub-distance and foreign-country checks — but only when `--regions NSW,VIC,QLD,SA,WA,TAS,ACT,NT`
is passed. `region_default: "Australia"` deletes `australia` from the gate's foreign-country
blocklist; `new zealand`, `united states` and `usa` stay in it, which is what we want.

**The bleed to expect.** The UK run measured US service-area pins arriving in a `country=gb` pull
carrying UK region tags. `country=au` is a hint, not a filter. The footprint gate is doing primary
work here, not cleanup — never ship this list without it. Hub radius **1.0°** (the UK ran 0.75°;
Australian tiles are sparser).

## Qualification, in plain language

The Maps stage is deliberately **volume-safe, not precise**. It never drops a plausible trade firm;
the real ICP judgement is the downstream site-text adjudication (`adjudicate/PROMPT.md`, ported from
the UK run with the Australian vocabulary). Rules run in order and the first failure is the recorded
`drop_reason` — denies first, then the positive allow, then the floor. Same shape as the UK's
post-GATE-3 rule set; the Australian changes are marked.

1. **Hard entity deny (any tag).** Charities and hospital foundations, colleges, schools and TAFEs,
   hospitals and aged care, places of worship, funeral homes, real-estate agencies and property
   developers, law and insurance offices, conveyancers, smash repairers and car dealers. This is the
   only rule allowed to look at a *secondary* Google tag. `property management company` stays in
   the primary-only deny (rule 2), where the UK run moved it.
2. **Primary-identity deny.** Structural, civil and geotechnical engineers, building surveyors (in
   Australia the surveyor is the *certifier*), building and pest inspectors, hardware and concrete
   suppliers, plant hire, plumbers and drainage, removalists, architects, kitchen/bathroom fitters,
   skip bins, property managers — dropped **only when that is their primary Google category**.
   **GATE 3 check:** count rows primaried `Structural engineer` whose *name* carries an ICP token;
   Google is known to mis-primary small remediation firms this way and a recovery pass cannot
   resurrect a deny drop. **Not denied:** `concrete contractor` and `excavating contractor` — they
   are name-gated recovery types instead, because Australian slab-lifting and house-raising firms
   are routinely primaried that way.
3. **Name deny.** The exact-match fallback: hospital/research/community foundations, councils,
   churches, RSL and surf clubs, smash repair and panel beating, the national hardware chains
   (Bunnings, Mitre 10, Bowens, Reece…), the concrete and quarry suppliers (Boral, Hanson, Holcim,
   readymix), Kennards and Coates hire, consulting-engineer and geotechnical practices, pre-purchase
   building and pest inspectors, termite treatment, drain-only jargon, plumbing-and-gas, real
   estate, strata, removalists. No bare `concrete`, `engineering`, `pest` or `termite`.
4. **Positive allow (any tag) — PROVISIONAL.** A row must carry at least one of: foundation ·
   underpin · restump · reblock · house raising · house levelling/leveling · concrete
   levelling/leveling · slab · structural · piling · pile driving · pier · helical · shoring · soil
   stabilisation · masonry · stonemason · building restoration · remedial. **The probe's type
   distribution replaces this list at GATE 1.** `waterproofing` is deliberately absent (bathroom
   trade); a basement specialist comes back by name.
5. **Review floor 5**, labelled `too_small`. A ghost-listing filter, not a size gate. **Revisit
   after the probe** using the median review count of the three probe tiles.

### The deliberate calls

- **Restumping and reblocking are ICP.** They are the Victorian word for the same job. If the
  operator rules them adjacent instead, only the `icp_type` tag in `gen-runsheet-au.js` changes.
- **Generic construction and concrete types are refused here and recovered by name.**
  `recover-generic-au-config.json` takes back `Construction company`, `Contractor`, `Concrete
  contractor`, `Excavating contractor`, `Waterproofing service`, `Pest control service`,
  `Carpenter` and the rest when the **name** carries an Australian ICP token (underpin, restump,
  reblock, relevel, house raising, slab lift, resin inject, subsidence, foundation, footing, piling,
  pier, stabilis-, structural, remedial, basement, helical, screw pile, stump) or a brand-family
  term. It can never resurrect a deny.
- **Unrated listings are a track, not junk.** `recover-unrated-au-config.json` (clone of the UK
  P7 pass) takes back rows with a blank `review_count` and a website.
- **No chain drop, ever.** Franchise licensees are kept and flagged (`brand_family` +
  `location_count`). Under the licence-registry owner rung a licensee is usually a named individual,
  which makes these rows *better* targets than in the UK.
- **No government deny.** Same reasoning as the UK; the institutional risk is covered by rules 1
  and 3.
- **No `website exists` rule.** Australian owner-operated trades are heavy Facebook-page-only users;
  they are the STEP 5d recovery track.
- **Pest control is not denied by type.** A restumper who repairs termite-damaged bearers may carry
  it as a secondary tag. Pure termite firms die at the allow, and the compounds are name-denied.

## Target role — the Australian title mapping

The offer and the buyer are unchanged; only the titles differ. Source:
`clients/atlas-growth/owner-prompts/foundation-repair-roles.md`, with the UK mapping in `ICP-uk.md`
as the nearer template.

### KEEP — can say yes to a Facebook lead-gen retainer

| Australian title | maps to the US bucket | why |
|---|---|---|
| **Director · Managing Director · Company Director** | Owner / President | The ASIC title. At an owner-operated firm the director *is* the marketing budget. |
| **Owner · Proprietor · Sole Trader · Founder · Principal** | Owner | Straight across. Sole traders are common; the business name is often the person's name. |
| **Licensee · Licence holder · Nominee / Nominated supervisor** (when the licence is in the person's own name, or their surname is in the business name) | Owner | The state licence board names the individual who holds the licence. For a 3-to-10-person firm that is the owner. A *company* licence with a nominee who is not a director is `gm`, not owner. |
| **Partner** | Partner | Straight across. |
| **General Manager · Operations Manager · Branch Manager** | GM | A P&L holder at an independent or a franchise branch. Deliberately kept. |
| **Marketing Manager · Head of Marketing** | Marketing | The buyer, or the champion who takes it to the director, at a mid-size firm. |
| **Sales Manager · Business Development Manager** | Sales manager | At this size a revenue owner, not the in-home closer. Keep, rank below director. |

### EXCLUDE — cannot authorise, and will be mistaken for the buyer

**Estimator · Inspector · Assessor · Building Inspector · Consultant (field)** · Structural Engineer
(unless also a director) · Site Supervisor · Supervisor · Site Manager · Project Manager · Contracts
Manager · Foreman · Leading Hand · Technician · Installer · Operative · Labourer · Apprentice ·
Scheduler · Office Manager (secondary only) · Receptionist · Bookkeeper · Accounts · HR · Safety
Manager · any "<Department> Manager" other than General or Marketing.

**Decisions made here:**

- **Estimator / Inspector / Assessor: EXCLUDE.** The Australian twin of the US `Estimator` and the
  UK `Surveyor` trap. The offer sells *inspection appointments*, and an Australian underpinning firm
  sends an "assessor" or "inspector" to do the free inspection. They consume the appointments; they
  do not buy them. Highest-risk false positive on this list.
- **Nominee supervisor: KEEP as owner only with a second signal.** QBCC and VBA licences held by a
  company name a *nominee*; at a small firm that is the owner, at a franchise branch it can be an
  employee. Surname-in-business-name or a director record makes them `owner_or_partner`; otherwise
  `gm`.
- **Site supervisor / contracts manager: EXCLUDE as a target**, last-resort contact only.

**The two rules that make this work are unchanged:** evaluate EXCLUDE before KEEP, and a department
qualifier demotes a title.

## Owner-finding note specific to Australia

There is **no Companies House**. ASIC's officer data is paid and behind Cloudflare — out of scope.
The registry substitute is the **state licence boards**: the licence holder is a named individual,
and NSW even has a dedicated licence class ("Underpinning and piering"). Probed 2026-09-20 from this
egress (outputs in the run's `RUN-NOTES.md`):

| registry | state | status | rung |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fair Trading `verify.licence.nsw.gov.au` | NSW | **live keyless JSON API, CONFIRMED from plain curl** (`POST /publicregisterapi/api/v1/licence/search/advQuery`, `pageSize` must be ≤ 10 — larger silently returns `[]`; filter by licence class `HBS_CON_Underpinning and Piering` → **122 current contractor licences**, individuals named directly; `GET .../details/{licenceType}/{licenceId}` for the record) | Tier 0 |
| VBA `bams.vba.vic.gov.au` | VIC | `find-practitioner` is Cloudflare 403; the BAMS Salesforce community answers 200 (Aura POST, needs a capture) | Tier 0 / Tier 3 |
| QBCC online licence search | QLD | proxy refuses the CONNECT (502); the QBCC root answers | Firecrawl or another egress |
| CBS `OccLicPubReg` | SA | 200, but a **reCAPTCHA** sits before the form — not the plain form the handoff hoped | session cookie from an operator solve, or skip |
| Building and Energy | WA | the search app is on `commerce.wa.gov.au/.../building-and-energy-licence-search` | to probe |
| ABN Lookup JSON | national | connection reset through this proxy; needs a free registered GUID anyway; no officers | name/status only |

Plan: NSW first (the API is the cheapest and names the licence class), then VIC, then QLD, then WA;
SA only if the operator hands over a solved session. Each registry gets a job-side
`registry_<state>.py` with a 3-call probe pasted in `RUN-NOTES.md` before any bulk pull, and its
output is fed to `prep-owner-batches.js` through `--ch` so `owner-prompt.md`'s registry rule applies
unchanged. Then site text (`fetch-sites.js`, plain → retry → Firecrawl residue), Haiku owner reads
with `owner-prompts/au-foundation-repair.md`, then the LinkedIn-restricted sweep for what is still
unnamed. **If fewer than three registries yield, expect the named rate to fall from the UK's 68%
toward the site-plus-sweep ceiling of ~45%.**

`site_l2_keywords` in the config carries the Australian contact-page paths (`get-a-quote`,
`book-an-inspection`, `free-inspection`, `enquire`) because on-site harvest is the only free email
rung on this scrape (no Maps email column).
