# Atlas Growth — foundation repair: decision-maker role set (from the owner prompt that ran the reader)

KEEP (five buckets, priority order):

| bucket | titles | why |
|---|---|---|
| owner_or_partner | owner, co-owner, founder, president, proprietor, principal, partner, CEO; **UK:** managing director, company director, director | the budget holder at an owner-operator shop |
| gm | general manager, branch manager, operations manager, VP, COO; **UK:** contracts manager, operations director | at dealer/branch networks the branch GM controls local lead spend |
| marketing | marketing manager or director | often the better first door for a lead-gen offer |
| sales_manager | sales manager, director of sales, inside sales manager; **UK:** sales director, commercial director, business development manager | they own what happens to a booked inspection |
| office_manager | secondary only | a reachable human when nothing above is found |

EXCLUDE: estimator, foundation technician, installer, crew lead, foreman, laborer, apprentice, field inspector, structural technician, production manager, project manager, service technician, dispatcher, scheduler, CSR, design specialist, home performance advisor, controller, accountant, bookkeeper, recruiter, purchasing manager. Estimator is the most common false positive (the offer sells "inspections").
Also excluded here as non-decision-makers: surveyor, quantity surveyor, site manager, structural engineer (unless also a director).

Entity rules: reject a contact whose result names a different city than the lead; reject the parent company's officers for a branch; reject departed people ("former CEO"); a name never contains a trade word.
